            याद का दीया · a diya on the water

            Float a diya for someone

            At the ghat, diyas float out on leaf boats for those we remember: grandparents, ancestors, anyone you miss tonight. Their name stays on the water each time you come back. It stays in this browser only.

            मिठाई का डिब्बा · a box of sweets

            Fill the box (up to six)
            The box

            न्योता भेजें · invite someone

            Invite someone to your village

            You get an invite card to send with the link. When they open it, the photo is the cover of their welcome, they're greeted by name, and their name is written in fireworks over the village. Their name, yours and a small copy of the photo travel inside the link, so only send it to them.
