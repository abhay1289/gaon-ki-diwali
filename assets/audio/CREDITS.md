## Background song

`ram-aayenge-chorus.m4a` is a 75-second cut of the chorus of *Ram Aayenge* (T-Series), taken from a video file supplied for this project (3:33.4 to 4:48.9, faded in and out, loudness-normalised). The song is copyrighted by its owners and is used here for personal, local use only. Before publishing this site, get permission from the rights holder, or remove the file and rely on the YouTube player, which plays the song through YouTube's licensed embed.
